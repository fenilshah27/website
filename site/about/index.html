What I believe

Progress has depended not only on visionary people, but on the institutions they built.

I believe that while individuals have the power to introduce new ideas, institutions are what enable those ideas to endure, evolve, and create lasting impact. Throughout history, the progress of societies has depended not only on visionary people but also on the institutions they built to preserve knowledge, cultivate leadership, and serve future generations.

This belief shapes the way I approach every project I undertake. Whether working in research, education, governance, publishing, or youth engagement, my objective is not simply to create successful initiatives, but to build institutions that remain relevant, adaptable, and valuable long after their founders have stepped away.
