The work

Designing, building and strengthening institutions.

My work is centred around institutions that contribute to education, research, governance, and public dialogue. While each initiative serves a distinct purpose, they are united by a shared belief: lasting impact is created through institutions that continue to evolve beyond the individuals who establish them.

Chapter one

Institution Building

Independent platforms that bring researchers, educators, students and young people together.

ACRI.
President

Ahmedabad Centre for Research and Innovation

An independent institution dedicated to strengthening research culture, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, and supporting knowledge driven development.

ACRI serves as a platform for researchers, students, educators, and professionals to collaborate through academic publishing, conferences, research initiatives, educational programmes, and institutional partnerships. Its long term vision is to contribute towards a stronger research ecosystem that encourages curiosity, innovation, and meaningful scholarly engagement.

Academic publishingConferencesResearch initiativesEducational programmesInstitutional partnerships
ICOY.
Director General

International Conference on Youth

A global platform that brings together young people, educators, researchers, policymakers, and institutions to exchange ideas, encourage collaboration, and address issues shaping the future of society.

ICOY seeks to create meaningful dialogue that extends beyond conferences by building long term international networks, partnerships, and collaborative opportunities.

Global dialogueInternational networksPartnerships

Chapter two

Research and Academic Publishing

International, peer reviewed journals committed to rigour, ethics and accessibility.

IJBMRS.
Chairperson

International Journal of Business and Management Research Studies

An international peer reviewed academic journal dedicated to advancing research in business, management, entrepreneurship, finance, economics, and related disciplines.

The journal is committed to maintaining rigorous editorial standards, ethical publishing practices, and meaningful scholarly contribution while providing researchers with an accessible and internationally oriented platform.

BusinessManagementEntrepreneurshipFinanceEconomics
IJELF.
Chairperson

International Journal of Education and Learning Futures

An international peer reviewed journal focused on research and innovation in education, pedagogy, curriculum, educational technology, assessment, and learning sciences.

The journal encourages interdisciplinary scholarship and supports conversations that explore the future of education and learning across diverse contexts.

PedagogyCurriculumEducational technologyAssessmentLearning sciences
